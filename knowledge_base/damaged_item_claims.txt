Damaged-Item Claim Process

A customer who receives a damaged item should report the issue as soon as possible and keep the product, packaging, invoice, and included accessories. Clear photographs or other evidence of the damage may be requested so that the support team can assess the claim.

The support team may arrange a return or replacement when the claim is approved and the product is eligible. Customers should avoid using a damaged item after discovering the issue because continued use can affect the assessment of the claim.
