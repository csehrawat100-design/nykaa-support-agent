Size-Exchange Policy

Eligible Apparel and Footwear items may be exchanged for another available size when the product qualifies for exchange and the requested size is in stock. The item should normally be unused, unworn, and retain its original tags and packaging.

A size exchange is subject to the product's exchange eligibility and the applicable request window. If the desired size is unavailable, the customer may need to use an eligible return and place a new order instead.
