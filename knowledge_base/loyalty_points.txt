Loyalty-Points Redemption Policy

Eligible loyalty points can be redeemed against qualifying purchases when the account and order meet the applicable redemption conditions. The number of points required and the maximum amount that can be redeemed may vary according to the current redemption rules.

Points may not be redeemable on every product, promotion, or transaction. Customers should check the redemption information shown during checkout and ensure that sufficient eligible points are available before completing the order.
